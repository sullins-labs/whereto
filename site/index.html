Where the real numbers come from

Each factor maps to a named public source with a known refresh cadence and licence. Ring-level values are derived from the anchor using published density, lot-size and travel-time distributions. Political readings are descriptive measures of enacted law and recorded voting, not endorsements, and they are scored only as distance from what you asked for.

Federal statistical products have become less dependable: NOAA retired its billion-dollar disasters series after 2024, BLS cut consumer price collection in several cities, and FEMA took the National Risk Index application offline while leaving the data downloadable. Every source is snapshotted at ingest and never deleted, so a series that disappears upstream still survives here.

Scoring runs entirely in your browser. Your income, household, politics and priorities are never sent anywhere.
